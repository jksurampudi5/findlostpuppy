FindLostPuppy

Privacy Policy · Effective date: September 15, 2026

In simple terms: FindLostPuppy is a community platform for sharing information about lost and found dogs. We collect only the information needed to provide these features, protect users and operate the service.

1. About FindLostPuppy

FindLostPuppy helps communities report lost dogs, share sightings and information, and support reunification. FindLostPuppy does not take custody of animals, determine legal ownership, or resolve ownership disputes.

2. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you create or use an account, we may collect your email address and profile information that you choose to provide, such as your name and contact details.

Dog and report information

You may provide information about a dog, including its name, breed, photographs, identifying characteristics, and whether it has been reported lost or found.

Location information

If you choose to provide location information, we may process approximate location and the location details you voluntarily enter to help people discover relevant lost-dog reports. Device location is used only with your permission. You can choose manual location information instead of device location where the app provides that option.

Photos and community content

Photos, sightings and other information you voluntarily submit may be stored and displayed to other users as part of the community service.

Technical information

The service may process limited technical information necessary for authentication, security, troubleshooting and reliable operation.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To publish and display lost-dog reports and community sightings.
  • To support location-based discovery and nearby recovery information when enabled or provided by you.
  • To communicate about your use of the service when necessary.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot the service.
  • To improve the reliability and functionality of FindLostPuppy.

4. How Information Is Shared

Information you submit as community content may be visible to other FindLostPuppy users so they can help identify and locate a lost dog. We do not sell your personal information.

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf, such as authentication, database, storage and hosting providers. They receive only the information reasonably necessary to provide their services and are expected to protect it appropriately.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the safety, rights and security of users, the service, or others.

5. Privacy and Contact Protection

FindLostPuppy is designed to limit unnecessary exposure of personal contact information in public community views. Where the application masks or limits contact details, those protections are intended to reduce unwanted exposure; however, no internet service can guarantee absolute security or privacy.

6. Location Privacy

Location is optional and should be provided carefully. We encourage users to share only the approximate location information needed to help recover a dog and not to publish sensitive information such as a precise home address unless they knowingly choose to do so.

7. Photos and User Content

You are responsible for content you submit. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to share, content that exposes another person's private information unnecessarily, or content that is unlawful, abusive, fraudulent, or misleading.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and support legitimate operational needs. If you want your account or personal information deleted, please contact us using the contact method provided in the application or the official FindLostPuppy project contact channel. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. These measures may include authenticated access, database access controls and protected storage. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

FindLostPuppy is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the service, please contact us so appropriate action can be considered.

11. Third-Party Services

FindLostPuppy may rely on third-party infrastructure and services to provide authentication, database, storage, hosting, analytics or other technical functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

12. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to provide optional information.
  • You can control device location permission through your device settings.
  • You can choose what dog photos and community information you submit.
  • You can request deletion or correction of information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published. Continued use of FindLostPuppy after an updated policy becomes effective means you acknowledge the updated policy.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, requests concerning your personal information, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the official FindLostPuppy contact method provided in the application or the project owner’s official contact channel.

15. Important Service Disclaimer

FindLostPuppy is a community information platform. It does not guarantee that a lost dog will be located or reunited, verify every report, or establish ownership of an animal. Users remain responsible for their own decisions, safety and interactions with other users. Please do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
